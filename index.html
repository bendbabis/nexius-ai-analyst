    Benchmark agents

    Two agents keep the market side of the twin alive: a collector that searches the web through Claude and returns sourced figures you can push into the parameters, and a profile panel that turns comparator quotes into a price position for Nexius.

    Agent 1 — benchmark collector (Claude with web search)

    Results appear here: each figure with its source, date and a button to push it into the matching parameter. Requires an API key with the web-search tool enabled; each run performs up to eight searches.

    no benchmark loaded

    Agent 2 — profile panel and comparator quotes

    Twelve profiles inside the Nexius underwriting box. Quotes come from a compliant source: Vilavi's own comparator data (LCA), a comparator panel subscription, or mystery shopping run by a person with a real identity. Import them as CSV (columns: profile_id, comparator, insurer, formula, price_monthly_ttc, fees_annual, rank); add Nexius as an insurer to measure its position.

    no quotes

    Why no scraping robot: LeLynx, LesFurets and Assurland forbid automated querying in their terms, protect their forms against bots, and a quote requires a real identity and consent; a robot entering invented identities would breach their terms and consumer law, and Belfius could not use the result. Vilavi operates a comparator and published LCA statistics in the Atelier Offre; that data, a panel bought from a comparator, or human mystery shopping are the routes the twin supports.

    Partner view — does the book work for Vilavi?

    The same book seen from the MGA: commissions and client fees against acquisition, servicing and claims-handling costs. A partnership that loses money for the partner is not a stable partnership; the costs here are Accenture assumptions to be replaced by Vilavi's figures (Q-53).
