Three steps.

  1. Enter your Anthropic API key above. It is sent only to api.anthropic.com and never written to a file.
  2. Load the Scenario Tool. The app reads the dashboard, scenarios, inputs, P&L, reserves, capital, IFRS 17 and the Challenge Book, draws the charts and loads the what-if engine with the workbook's assumptions.
  3. Ask. Claude answers from the workbook's values and from any what-if you run; it says when something is not in them.
